What is Artistic Commerce?

// FILE 001 — LOGLINE

Somewhere past the last exit of the known galaxy, the starship Artistic Commerce drifts between dying star systems — half cargo hauler, half art collective, entirely behind on rent.

Its crew of washed-up visionaries, sellout geniuses, and one alarmingly sincere intern are on an impossible mission: to make something real in a universe engineered to sell you a slightly worse copy of everything you already love.

It's a show about creativity, authenticity, and the deeply stupid, deeply beautiful things people do to protect them. Think Spaceballs raised by Monty Python in a squat full of broken synthesizers. Cosmic, grungy, and allergic to taking itself seriously — while quietly meaning every word.
