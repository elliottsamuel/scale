# ARTISTIC COMMERCE — promo site

A standalone promotional landing page for **Artistic Commerce**, an original
sci-fi comedy series. This is its own project and shares nothing with the
`SCALE` app at the repository root — it just happens to live in the same repo,
in its own folder.

## The show, in one line

A ragtag crew of alien artists — from a utopia that got bored of itself — point
their held-together-with-tape starship, the *Artistic Commerce*, at Earth to
help usher along its rebirth (and enjoy the party). Each episode, they tap into
one human consciousness to read how that person actually sees the world.

The idea underneath it: **business can be an expression of art** — an answer to
the hustle, and a resolution of the shame around commerce.

## Running it

One self-contained file, no build step or dependencies.

- **Open locally:** open `index.html` in any browser.
- **Host it:** serve this folder statically. On GitHub Pages it lives at
  `/<repo>/artistic-commerce/`.

## Editing the copy

All copy lives in `index.html`. Sections, in order:

1. **Hero** — wordmark (editable SVG text lockup), tagline, premise lede.
2. **The Idea** — the manifesto (business as art).
3. **The Ship & The Crew** — the premise, plus a decorative "ship's ledger."
4. **The Device** — the consciousness mechanic and the misfire cold-open format.
5. **The First Five** — episodes AC-101 → AC-105 as a catalog of "signals."
6. **Still Being Built** — the open questions.
7. **Get Alerts** — email capture (front-end only; wire up a backend to store).

The featured episode subjects are real public figures presented as the
affectionate subjects of a fictional show; the footer carries a short line
noting no endorsement or participation. Episode 5 is intentionally left open.

## Design

A committed single "poster" look: risograph-print grunge (fluoro pink, volt
green, federal blue on inky violet-black newsprint), film grain, scanlines,
and a stock-ticker marquee — the sci-fi-broadcast-meets-DIY-zine collision that
mirrors the *art vs. commerce* tension in the title.
