The Insight

Support arrives all at once at diagnosis.

Medicine is organized around events. So is everyone around it.

“Support arrives all at once at diagnosis. Then everyone goes back to their lives.”

Director of Child Life, UCLA
The Scale
02/ 05

The sickest children spend more time in the hospital than everyone else combined.

Medically complex kids — the ones whose treatment lasts years, not days. And there are more of them every year, because medicine keeps getting better at saving them.

The Thesis
05/ 05

The greatest opportunity in medicine is in the waiting.

Everything we know about how people heal points at the time we’ve designed the least.

Empowerment

Across 33,000 patients, those most engaged in their own care cost 31% less and scored better on 9 of 13 health outcomes.

Expression

Adults given identical wounds who wrote about their feelings 20 minutes a day: 76% fully healed by day 11.

Immersion

In a randomized trial of children, how absorbed a child was in an experience directly predicted how much less pain they reported.

Connection

Across 148 studies and 308,849 people, strong relationships predicted a 50% higher likelihood of survival.

Laughter

A meta-analysis of 912 children found play significantly reduced anxiety — in the children and their parents.
