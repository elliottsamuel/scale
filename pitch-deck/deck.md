# Investor Deck — The Case

Slides 01–05. The argument moves **Insight → Scale → Cost → Opportunity → Thesis**.

---

## 01 · The Insight

# Support arrives all at once at diagnosis.

*Medicine is organized around events. So is everyone around it.*

> "Support arrives all at once at diagnosis. Then everyone goes back to their lives."
>
> — **Director of Child Life, UCLA**

---

## 02 · The Scale

# The sickest children spend more time in the hospital than everyone else combined.

*Medically complex kids — the ones whose treatment lasts years, not days. And there are more of them every year, because medicine keeps getting better at saving them.*

- They are fewer than **1% of American children** — but they account for **54% of all days children spend in hospital beds.**

---

## 03 · The Cost

# They are the most expensive patients in pediatrics.

*Under 1% of children. A third of everything spent on children's health.*

- US pediatric hospitalizations cost **$36.3 billion a year.** These children account for **57% of it — roughly $20 billion.**

---

## 04 · The Opportunity

# What if you could shorten these stays by 10%?

*Not with a drug. Not with a device. With the hours nobody has ever tried to use.*

- Conservatively, **$300–500 million a year** in avoided hospital days — before counting readmissions, where these children are **71% of all unplanned returns.**
- The average hospitalized patient receives about **22 minutes of direct bedside care a day.** They spend **75% of the day in their room, 65% of it alone.**

> **What if the greatest untapped opportunity for healing lives inside patient downtime?**

---

## 05 · The Thesis

# The greatest opportunity in medicine is in the waiting.

*Everything we know about how people heal points at the time we've designed the least.*

- **Empowerment** — Across 33,000 patients, those most engaged in their own care cost **31% less** and scored better on **9 of 13** health outcomes.
- **Expression** — Adults given identical wounds who wrote about their feelings for 20 minutes a day: **76% fully healed by day 11.**
- **Immersion** — In a randomized trial of children, how absorbed a child was in an experience **directly predicted how much less pain** they reported.
- **Connection** — Across 148 studies and 308,849 people, strong relationships predicted a **50% higher likelihood of survival.**
- **Laughter** — A meta-analysis of 912 children found play significantly reduced anxiety — in the children **and their parents.**
