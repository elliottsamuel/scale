# 🌅🪶 Perch

**Arrive at a healthy vantage point.**

Perch is for anyone navigating a health scare, a healing journey, or caring
for someone they love. It helps you name the goal, feel your way into it, and
let that feeling guide the actions you take — instead of grinding through a
to-do list that never quite feels like progress.

## The ethos

Most goal apps start and end with tasks. Perch starts somewhere else: **the
feeling you're moving toward.** The idea is that once you can genuinely feel
what it's like to be on the other side of this goal — steady, free, whole,
whatever that word is for you — the right next action becomes obvious. Feel
first, then act from there.

## The three views

- **🌅 Vantage** — your home screen. The first time you open Perch, it asks
  for the biggest goal you're moving toward right now, for your own
  well-being, someone else's, or both. After that, every time you open the
  app, it reflects that goal straight back to you.
- **🌿 Plan** — a brain dump, not a sequential checklist. Capture every idea,
  action, and thing-you're-already-doing in service of the goal, in whatever
  order they come to you. Any item can become **its own goal** with one tap —
  so "rebuild strength" can branch off from "feel steady again" and get its
  own plan and its own feeling.
- **💗 Feel** — cultivate the feeling you imagine you'll have once you've
  reached the goal. Name it in a word or two, describe where it lives in your
  body, and take a guided 60-second embodiment pause. A bridge button carries
  that feeling straight into Plan, prompting your next action from it.

## Goal hierarchy

Your top goal is your vantage point. Any Plan item can branch into a
sub-goal, and sub-goals can branch again — so you end up with a tree that
mirrors how a big goal actually breaks down. The 🌳 button in the top bar
opens the full tree so you can jump to any goal, drill back up with the
breadcrumb trail, or start a completely separate top-level goal.

## Running it

A single self-contained file — no build step, no dependencies.

- **Open locally:** open `index.html` in any browser.
- **On your phone:** host the folder statically (e.g. GitHub Pages) and use
  "Add to Home Screen."

Everything — goals, plan items, feelings — lives on your device
(`localStorage`). Nothing is uploaded.

## Design

A dawn palette (coral, gold, sky blue) drawn from scratch: the idea of first
light breaking over a horizon, seen from a bird's perch on a branch. A warm
daybreak theme by default, with a calm dusk theme available via the ◐
button — deliberately unclinical for an app about health and healing.

## Roadmap ideas

- Reminders to revisit the Feel view, not just the Plan.
- Export / back up your goal tree; sync across devices.
- Optional AI reflection on your brain-dumped plan (mirrors the SCALE app's
  approach — a tiny backend keeps any API key private).
- Progress markers on the Vantage view once a goal's plan is mostly done.
